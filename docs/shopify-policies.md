## Refund & Exchange Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

Every piece from The Ritual Era is designed and made in small runs in our Mumbai studio. We want you to love what you receive, so we offer easy exchanges.

### Exchanges

- You can request an exchange for a different size or another piece of equal or higher value within **7 days of delivery**.
- Pieces must be unworn, unwashed and unaltered, with all original tags and packaging attached.
- To start an exchange, email info@theritualera.com or WhatsApp us on +91 97697 28791 with your order number and the piece you would like instead. We will share the return address or arrange a pickup where available.
- Once we receive and check the piece, we dispatch the replacement within 3–5 working days. If the replacement costs more, we will send you a link to pay the difference.
- If the size or piece you want is no longer available, we will issue store credit for the amount paid, valid for 12 months.

### Refunds
We do not offer refunds for change of mind. We refund to your original payment method only when:

- the piece arrives damaged or defective, or we sent you the wrong item — please tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the piece and its packaging; or
- you cancel your order before it has been dispatched; or
- we are unable to fulfil your order.

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days. Your bank or card provider may take a few more days to show the amount in your account.

### Pieces that cannot be exchanged

- Made-to-measure and customised pieces, which are made specially for you.
- Pieces bought on sale or with a discount code, unless they arrive damaged, defective or incorrect.
- Gift cards.

### Return shipping
We cover return shipping for damaged, defective or incorrect items. For exchanges of size or style, the cost of sending the piece back to us is yours, and we cover the cost of sending the replacement to you.

### Questions
Email info@theritualera.com or WhatsApp +91 97697 28791, 10am–7pm (closed on public holidays).

## Shipping Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

### Where we ship
We currently ship to addresses within India. For orders outside India, please write to us at info@theritualera.com before ordering.

### Shipping charges

- **Free shipping** on orders of ₹5,000 and above.
- A flat **₹150** on orders below ₹5,000.

The shipping charge is shown at checkout before you pay. All prices on our site include GST.

### Dispatch and delivery times

- In-stock pieces are dispatched within **3–5 working days** of your order. Many of our pieces are finished by hand, so please allow this time for us to prepare yours.
- Once dispatched, delivery usually takes 2–4 working days to metro cities and 4–7 working days elsewhere in India.
- Made-to-measure pieces have their own timeline, which we confirm with you before you order.

Delivery times are estimates. Delays caused by courier partners, weather, public holidays or other events outside our control can occur, and we will keep you informed.

### Tracking your order
When your order is dispatched, we email you the courier name and tracking number. You can also see your order status from the link in your order confirmation email.

### Damaged parcels and failed deliveries

- If your parcel arrives open or visibly damaged, please refuse it or photograph it before opening, and tell us within 48 hours of delivery.
- If a delivery fails because the address was incomplete or nobody was available after the courier's attempts, the parcel returns to us. We can re-send it once the re-shipping charge is paid.

### Questions
Email info@theritualera.com or WhatsApp +91 97697 28791, 10am–7pm (closed on public holidays).
